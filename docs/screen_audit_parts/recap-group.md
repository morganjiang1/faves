### Flows checked end to end (web)

- Host opens `/group/demo`, copies the invite link (1,115 characters); a fresh browser opens it, the group rebuilds from the link, the friend types "Riley", votes Maybe on a stop, taps "Send my votes back" and copies that link; the host opens it and Riley's vote merges in with everyone else's kept. A cut or made-up `d=` shows the broken-link state. No console errors or warnings.
- Recap: rate (thumbs + chips) → compare → five or six slides → share card in four colorways → Post your day (the post lands in `state.posts`, the recap is marked posted, the success modal opens). The web share falls back to the Partiful share sheet when the browser has no share sheet.
- Rank: pick a circle → up to 4 comparisons → ranked toast and back; first-ever ranking shows the modal.
- Unit tests: `src/lib/__tests__/group.test.ts` (encode, decode, broken links, merge, tallies, leader) and `src/lib/__tests__/ranking.test.ts` (binary insertion, caps, too tough, skip, undo, bucket merge, recap sort).
